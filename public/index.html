Frequently asked questions

Can I cancel any time?

Yes. Cancel from "My account" then "Manage subscription". No contract. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the paid month.

Does the AI actually generate good content?

It uses advanced AI models (Claude, by Anthropic) with instructions designed for tradespeople. The content adapts to your trade, your area and your services. Try it free and judge for yourself.

Is my data safe?

Your data is stored on secure servers (Supabase). Payments are handled by Stripe. We never sell or share your data. We comply with UK GDPR and are registered with the ICO.

Does it work for my trade?

It works for over 30 trades: builders, painters, plumbers, electricians, photographers, hairdressers, personal trainers, physiotherapists, mechanics and many more. If yours isn't listed, type it in manually.

Can I connect my WhatsApp?

Yes, it's already available on the Pro plan. Connect your WhatsApp Business without leaving the app on your phone. The AI analyses new messages and suggests replies. Nothing is sent without your approval.

What if I don't like it?

You can try it free with no card. If after paying you're not happy, you have 14 days to request a full refund.

Do I need to be tech-savvy?

No. If you can use WhatsApp, you can use OficioAI. Enter your details, AI prepares everything and you copy and paste.
